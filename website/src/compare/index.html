How FlareLens compares

FlareLens is an open-source Platform-as-a-Service whose control plane runs in your own Cloudflare account and whose workloads run on your own servers. Here is how it stacks up—including where the alternatives are the better choice.

When to pick it You want PaaS workflows (fleet, deploys, mesh, logs, secrets, backups) on sovereign servers at $0 control-plane cost. You want zero Linux hosts to manage and accept PaaS compute & bandwidth pricing. You want 200+ 1-click self-hosted OSS app templates with built-in Nixpacks image builds. You want pure stateless CLI deploys over SSH with zero persistent control plane. You have 50+ engineers needing custom CRDs, HPA autoscaling, and multi-team RBAC namespaces. You need SOC2 vendor-managed APM traces, RUM, and PagerDuty on-call suites out of the box.
